REAL HELP. REAL CHOICES.

Have you ever wondered if vaping is affecting you?

  • Is it getting in the way of school, work or sleep?
  • Is it causing problems?
  • Do I feel anxious or upset when I can’t do it?
  • Am I doing it a lot?

If you answered YES to any of these questions vaping might be taking more control than you think.
Quitting or cutting back on vaping isn’t easy and you don’t have to do it alone. Connect with youth-friendly resources like Quash, Kids Help Phone, and Talk Substance for support, practical tips and tools to protect your mental health while taking back control.
