Quick Facts

Vaping can negatively affect mental health.

Vapes can contain high levels of nicotine and cannabis, increasing the risk of addiction.

Youth are targeted with candy and fruit-flavoured e-liquid. Don’t be tricked.

Vapes can create toxic waste that harms the environment.

Whether someone is vaping nicotine or cannabis, there is support available to help youth reduce or quit.

What are vapes?

They come in many shapes and sizes from box-like devices with mouthpieces to smaller pens.

The Process

  1. The substance is heated into an aerosol and inhaled.
  2. The chemical particles quickly enter the bloodstream and circulate throughout the body, including the brain.
  3. Any remaining aerosol is exhaled.

They are using the same marketing tactics—like flavours, influencer endorsements, lifestyle advertising, and packaging—that were successful in addicting generations of people to commercial tobacco products. These tactics are designed to addict a whole new generation to nicotine.

The influence of tobacco companies now extends beyond nicotine products.

Major tobacco corporations have invested heavily in cannabis companies whose vape products are sold in Canada, including through the Ontario Cannabis Store (OCS). They are involved in the sale of cannabis vape products and related technologies. This raises concern that the same industry that profited from nicotine addiction is now positioning itself within the growing cannabis vape market.

Is it safe?

E-cigarettes, e-liquid and cartridges/pods in Canada are controlled by federal and provincial restrictions to help people understand the risks and make informed choices.
In Ontario, all vaping products (nicotine or cannabis) must have clear labels. Packaging must show health warnings and identify the product and its strength/concentration.
