TOOLS TO GUIDE, RESOURCES TO SHARE

Not An Experiment supports educators with classroom tools, activities, and guidance to foster open conversations and protect students from the harms of vaping.

School Wide Resources

Legend:

Nicotine Cannabis General Vaping

Training

Self-paced learning resources designed to support educators to learn about vaping and how to talk with youth.

  • Adult E-Learning Module
  • Brief Conversations Toolkit

Activities

Online escape room where players gather clues and generate a report on the harms of vaping nicotine.

  • Not An Experiment Online Game
  • Online Game Answer Key

Escape the Experiment

Downloadable, print-ready escape room style game. Escape the Experiment teaches youth about the risks of vaping, nicotine addiction, and industry marketing tactics.

  • Teacher Facilitation Guide
  • Student-Led Facilitation Guide
  • Discussion Guide
  • Escape the Experiment Activity

Operation Truth

Downloadable, print-ready detective style game. Operation Truth teaches youth about the harms of vaping cannabis, how high levels of THC can negatively affect mental health and strategies on how to stand up to the pressure to vape.

  • Teacher Facilitation Guide
  • Discussion Guide
  • Answer Key
  • Operation Truth Activity

Grade 7 & 8 Lesson Plans

A series of three lessons for students in grades 7 or 8, with links to the Ontario Physical Education and Health Curriculum – Healthy Living

  • Grade 7 & 8 Lesson Plans

Not An Experiment Trivia

Downloadable, print ready trivia game. Not An Experiment Trivia engages students while they learn about the effects of vaping nicotine and cannabis.

  • Trivia Game Cards
  • Instruction Guide

Refusal Skills Activity

An educational resource containing interactive activities that can be used to give students the opportunity to practice coping with pressures to vape. Its aim is to build students’ confidence and ability to make informed choices.

  • Refusal Skills Activity
